# Scenario — research hypothesis

## Target capability

Distinguish observation from explanation and design an experiment that separates competing hypotheses.

## Setup

Provide an experiment result with at least two plausible explanations.

Example structure:

- model performance improves after adding a context-compression module;
- hypothesis A: useful information is being preserved more selectively;
- hypothesis B: the change merely alters effective context length or compute.

## Delegate region

- plotting;
- formatting;
- routine metric extraction;
- experiment boilerplate.

## Core region

- observation/hypothesis separation;
- falsifiable prediction;
- discriminating experiment;
- update after evidence.

## Evaluation

Look for:

- whether the system prevents "result → story" collapse;
- whether the learner states a prediction before new evidence;
- whether the proposed experiment actually distinguishes hypotheses;
- whether the conclusion is updated rather than defended.
