# Skill-first dogfooding runbook

Dogfooding should feel like normal learning. The evaluation layer observes decisive moments; it must not make the learner operate a protocol.

## Private evidence

Use .dogfooding/ for private real-session notes when local files are useful. Keep identifiable details, private source material, and raw transcripts out of the public repository.

Usually record a minimized description rather than the full conversation.

## Start an arc

Choose a real capability the learner cares about.

Record:

- target capability;
- initial frontier hypothesis;
- relevant learner self-report;
- what would count as meaningful later retrieval/application/transfer.

Do not pre-fill success.

## During a session

Use skills/ablearc/SKILL.md normally.

Capture only decisive events:

- the cognitive move AbleArc selected;
- what the learner actually did;
- what changed in the learner model;
- what remained uncertain;
- an awkward or incorrect teaching decision;
- whether a companion Skill was used and why.

Do not interrupt every exchange to collect evidence.

## Across sessions

A later session should depend on what happened earlier.

When useful, test retrieval before replay. Change one meaningful dimension when testing transfer: representation, context, assumptions, or surface form.

Time passing alone is not evidence of forgetting.

## Companion-Skill evaluation

For Archify, ask whether the diagram exposed structure the learner could then trace, predict, or reconstruct.

For University Skill, ask whether the topic-first textbook/coursebook became a useful reference for subsequent active learning.

For youtube-render-pdf / bilibili-render-pdf, ask whether source-faithful notes reduced friction and whether the learner could later explain, apply, compare, or retrieve material from the lecture.

Artifact quality matters, but artifact existence is not mastery evidence.

## Promotion gate

Do not add a new general rule to skills/ablearc/SKILL.md because one conversation was awkward.

Prefer a core change when:

1. the failure repeats across independent real sessions; or
2. it is clearly structural and high consequence.

Before promoting it:

- classify the failure;
- test whether a clearer instruction or existing companion can solve it;
- add or update a distinguishing scenario in tests/SKILL-BEHAVIOR.md;
- choose the smallest fix.

## Completion

A useful dogfood arc has real evidence from more than one interaction, continuity with earlier context, at least one meaningful retrieval/application/transfer opportunity when appropriate, and explicit unresolved uncertainty.

If the evidence is weak, record insufficient evidence rather than inventing progress.
